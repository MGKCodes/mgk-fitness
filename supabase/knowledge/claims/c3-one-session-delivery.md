title: One session a week is a poor way to deliver a week's volume
confidence: Moderate — a practical corollary, not a directly tested finding
last_checked: 2026-08-20
sources:
  - https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/view/460
depends_on:
  - PlanShape two-exposure rule
---

A muscle needing 12-20 hard sets a week is badly served by one session, because
sets late in a long session are performed under accumulated fatigue and
contribute less than the same set fresh.

This is reasoning rather than a result, and it is the honest justification for
the rule the app enforces — **not** c2-frequency-volume-equated, which says
frequency itself is close to neutral.

**If this is wrong:** the two-exposure rule becomes an arbitrary constraint that
rejects otherwise fine plans, a bro split being the obvious case. It is stricter
than c2 justifies on its own, so it is the first thing to revisit if anybody
complains.
