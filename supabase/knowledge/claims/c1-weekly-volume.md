Hypertrophy follows weekly hard sets per muscle in a dose-response, with
diminishing returns. Around 12-20 sets per muscle per week is a reasonable
working range for a trained lifter.

The direction is consistent across the literature. The exact band is a summary
of noisy data and varies by muscle, by individual, and by what counts as a hard
set — so treat it as a guard rail rather than a prescription.

**If this is wrong:** the volume bounds are wrong and plans get accepted or
rejected on a bad number. The bounds are deliberately wide, so being somewhat
off is survivable.
