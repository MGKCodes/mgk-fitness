Rotating movements and keeping the same ones produce similar hypertrophy and
strength. Variation has value against staleness and overuse, but is not itself
a growth stimulus, and it works best planned rather than reactive.

Progressive overload is impossible on a movement you keep replacing, because
there is no previous number to beat. So main lifts do not rotate, and
accessories become eligible only after six sessions without improvement.

**If this is wrong** — if variation does drive growth — the rotation interval is
too conservative and slots should turn over faster.
