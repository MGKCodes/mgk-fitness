A lift that has not moved in several sessions is information, not a failure, and
the useful response depends on which lift it is.

**A stalled main lift is a programming question.** Load, fatigue, sleep, food,
stress, or simply a rep target being hit exactly rather than a true limit.
Swapping the movement hides it rather than fixing it, so do not offer a swap —
ask what has been going on, and consider holding the weight or backing off
before adding.

**A stalled accessory is a selection question.** It has done its job or it never
suited this person, and after six sessions without improvement it is worth
replacing with something that fills the same role.

Never present a stall as the lifter's fault. Six sessions at the same weight
during a hard month at work is a sensible outcome, not a lapse.
