Race mode. Choose Run or Race before you set off: a race counts down to the finish and says what time you are on for. Afterwards it gets a finish of its own: your official time, whether it is your first or fastest at the distance, and how you ran it, 5K by 5K. Races count as their own time in your records.

Also: splits fold into a chart with your fastest and slowest, kilometre markers keep clear of each other, and the coach and sign-in share Lift's design.
