automatically from the data you give it, and no clinician has reviewed them.

You are responsible for deciding whether any session is safe for you on the day.

**Talk to a doctor before starting or changing a training plan** if any of the
following apply: you have a heart condition, chest pain, dizziness or fainting;
high blood pressure; a bone, joint or muscle problem that exercise could worsen;
you are pregnant or recently gave birth; you are recovering from illness,

**Stop and seek medical attention if you feel unwell while exercising** — chest
