# History — none of this describes how Lift stands now

**Every document in this directory is out of date, deliberately, and none of
them is maintained.** They are kept for how something came to be. They sit in
their own directory so that the path is the warning: a stale plan one line away
from the live one gets read as current.

For what is left to do, go to [`../submission-week.md`](../submission-week.md).
For why the app is the way it is, [`../decisions/`](../decisions/).

## What is here

All four were frozen on 1 October 2026, as they were at `db88a4f`. Each opens
with what was already wrong in it that day. Only the banner and the link
targets were changed.

- **[release-2.0.0.md](release-2.0.0.md)** — the first release plan, from 17
  August. Read it for why Liftio is replaced, what compliance the old app had
  already paid for, and the case for owning receipt validation. Its payments
  phase describes a design that was dropped.
- **[submission-week-record.md](submission-week-record.md)** — the September
  plan for both submissions. Read it for the six blockers found by reading the
  code, and the four decisions behind the webhook. Its checklists are wrong.
- **[lift-2.0.0-punch-list.md](lift-2.0.0-punch-list.md)** — what the first
  TestFlight build turned up, August. Most of its screens have been rebuilt
  twice since.
- **[handover-2026-09-30.md](handover-2026-09-30.md)** — the handover into the
  redesign's planning session. Its branch, worktree and open questions are all
  gone or answered.

## What is not here, and why

**The redesign, the logging rework and the design review stayed in `docs/`.**
All three are finished, but the code and the screen board cite their decisions
by number (R1 to R13, D1 to D6, findings 1 to 19), and a document other things
treat as live is live. Each says at its top that it is closed.

## The rule

Nothing in here gets edited. If one of these is wrong about the past, that is
fixed by a note in the document that replaced it.
